Quick Sort, one step at a time

Watch how Quick Sort splits an array around a pivot. The pivot is always the first element of the current sub-array, and every move of i and j gets its own box with an explanation.

Separate numbers with commas or spaces. Use 2 to 16 numbers; negatives, decimals and repeats are fine.
How this version of Quick Sort works
  1. Take the sub-array from index low to high. The pivot is a[low].
  2. Put i at low + 1 and j at high.
  3. Move i right while a[i] ≤ pivot. It stops at the first element bigger than the pivot.
  4. Move j left while a[j] > pivot. It stops at the first element smaller than or equal to the pivot.
  5. If i < j, swap a[i] and a[j] and repeat from step 3.
  6. If i > j (they crossed), swap the pivot with a[j]. The pivot is now in its final place.
  7. Repeat the whole process on the left part and the right part of the pivot.
