Product of Array Except Self

LeetCode 238. For each position, find the product of all the other numbers, without division. Step through the two passes and watch res and p change.

How it works


    

  • The key identity. The product of everything except nums[i] equals (product of everything to the left of i) times (product of everything to the right of i). The two halves are called the prefix product and the suffix product.
    • 

  • Pass 1 (left to right). p holds the product of everything to the left of i. We store it in res[i] before multiplying nums[i] into p, so nums[i] never counts itself. The first position has nothing on its left, and an empty product is 1.
    • 

  • Pass 2 (right to left). We reset p to 1, and now it holds the product of everything to the right of i. Multiplying res[i] (the left part) by p (the right part) gives the final answer.
    • 

  • Why not divide? Dividing the total product by nums[i] breaks when the array contains zeros. The problem also forbids it.
    • 

  • Cost. O(n) time with two simple passes, and O(1) extra space, because res is the required output and the only other variable is p.
    • 



Edge cases to try


Load 1, 0, 3, 4 (one zero, so only the zero position gets a non-zero answer), 0, 0, 3 (two zeros, so everything is 0), -1, 2, -3 (negatives), and 5, 7 (the minimum length).
