Daily Temperatures: monotonic stack

LeetCode 739. Step through the solution and watch the stack, the temperatures, and the answers change.

Stack (bottom to top), stores day indices

ans array (days to wait)

for i in range(n):    while stack and temperatures[i] > temperatures[stack[-1]]:        prev = stack.pop()        ans[prev] = i - prev    stack.append(i)

The idea. The stack holds days still waiting for a warmer day. Each new day pops every colder day off the top and gives each one its answer, i - prev. Then it joins the stack itself.

Why O(n). Every day is pushed once and popped at most once, so the nested loop does at most 2n operations in total.

Edge cases to try. Load 70, 70, 70 (all equal, so all zeros), 60, 65, 70 (strictly increasing), and 90, 80, 70 (strictly decreasing, so the stack fills up).
