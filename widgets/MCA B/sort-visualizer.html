Sorting algorithms, step by step

Sorting means putting items in order. Pick an algorithm, press Play, or drag the slider to move through every step. Quick sort and merge sort use divide and conquer (split, solve, combine). Bubble, selection and insertion sort are simpler but slower. Heap sort uses a clever tree layout inside the array.
