Quick Sort with the last element as pivot

Watch the array move in the live animation, then read every move of i and j in its own box below. Each box explains what was compared and why.

Separate numbers with commas or spaces. Use 2 to 16 numbers; negatives, decimals and repeats are fine.
How this version of Quick Sort works
  1. Take the sub-array from index low to high. The pivot is the last element, a[high].
  2. Put i at low − 1, just before the sub-array. Everything from low up to i will be the "≤ pivot" zone, which starts empty.
  3. Move j from low to high − 1, checking one element at a time.
  4. If a[j] ≤ pivot: move i one step right, then swap a[i] and a[j]. This pulls the small element into the left zone.
  5. If a[j] > pivot: do nothing, just move j on.
  6. When j reaches the pivot, swap the pivot with a[i + 1]. The pivot is now in its final place.
  7. Repeat on the part left of the pivot and the part right of it.
